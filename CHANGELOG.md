# Changelog

## 1.5.0

### Quest Menu Integration
- Added an optional Candy Jar entry to Quest Menu when both mods are enabled.
- Added ordered objectives for finding and returning the Power Regulator,
  charging the Unstable Crystal, and receiving the Candy Jar.
- Added live crystal EXP progress and compact Pokégear text for Gold.
- Immersive leads now appear on Cinnabar Island and outside the Ruins of Alph.
- Long researcher and engineer dialogue now pauses naturally while scrolling.
- Fixed Gold rejecting the Engineer's Gen 1-shaped trainer registration.
- Candy Jar remains fully functional when Quest Menu is not installed.

## 1.4.0

### Gold Support

- Added Pokémon Gold compatibility.
- Moved the Gold researcher to the Ruins of Alph Research Center.
- Added a Gold-specific Power Plant Engineer placement.
- Categorized Candy Jar as a QOL mod with Gen 1 and Gen 2 support.

## 1.3.0

First public release.

### Candy Jar
- Added the Candy Jar custom menu.
- Added Store Candy functionality.
- Added Candify EXP functionality.
- Added research/progress display.

### Research Quest
- Added the Paldean researcher and research quest.
- Requires all 8 Gym Badges before the scientist offers the research.
- Added the Unstable Crystal.
- Added the Power Regulator objective.
- Added the Power Plant Engineer encounter and battle.
- Added the Power Regulator reward.
- Added research-progress dialogue.
- Added the final Candy Jar reward.

### Compatibility
- Fixed Candy Jar item handling so normal vanilla item-use behaviour is preserved for other inventory items.
